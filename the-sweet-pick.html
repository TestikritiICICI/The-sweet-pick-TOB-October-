    About The Orange Book

    The Orange Book is ICICI Bank’s personal finance publication — simple, practical ways to save, invest and protect your money.

    Festive season means more shopping, gifting, offers and impulse decisions. In the process, behavioural biases can quietly influence what feels like the “better” choice. This edition lets you experience five of them through simple mithai choices, so you can make more considered festive decisions.

    The five biases in this game first appeared in The Orange Book Vol. 17 (October 2022) and Vol. 30 (November 2023).

      A sweeter way to choose

      The festive season is a time for happiness, celebration and shopping. But biases can quietly find their way into our decisions and sometimes make us spend more than we planned.

      The idea is not to stop enjoying the season. It is simply to notice what may be shaping a choice, pause when it matters and strike the right balance, always.
