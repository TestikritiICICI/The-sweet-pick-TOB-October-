    About this edition

    Five festive biases, drawn from The Orange Book’s own back catalogue.

    Festive season means more shopping, gifting, offers and impulse decisions. In the process, behavioural biases can quietly influence what feels like the “better” choice.

    This edition lets you experience five of them through simple mithai choices, so you can make more considered festive decisions.

    The five biases here first appeared in The Orange Book Vol. 17 (October 2022) and Vol. 30 (November 2023).

    How to play
    1. Trust your gut and make your Sweet Pick.
    2. Flip only your choice to see what was at play.
    3. Keep shopping for your next box.

    Make your pick first. Then flip your chosen card to see what may have influenced it.

    A sweeter way to choose

      The festive season is a time for happiness, celebration and shopping. But biases can quietly find their way into our decisions and sometimes make us spend more than we planned.

      The idea is not to stop enjoying the season. It is simply to notice what may be shaping a choice, pause when it matters and strike the right balance, always.
